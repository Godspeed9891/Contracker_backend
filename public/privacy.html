ConTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

ConTracker is a container and order tracking platform used by bonded terminals, their staff, importers, agents and customs officers. This page explains what information the service keeps and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, organisation and role.
  • Identity document: a photo of your ID, uploaded when you register, so an administrator can verify who you are before your account is approved.
  • Work records: the jobs, documents, invoices, messages and activity entries you create or are given access to inside the platform.
  • Sign-in information: your password is stored only as a one-way hash. We also record the time and device type of your last sign-in.

How we use it

  • To create and verify your account and to let you sign in.
  • To show each person only the jobs and records they are permitted to see.
  • To send you the one-time verification code when you register.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Email and Google services

ConTracker sends verification codes from its own Gmail mailbox, using the Gmail API with the permission to send email only. ConTracker does not read, list or delete any email, and it does not access the Gmail or Google account data of its users. The only Google account involved is ConTracker's own sending mailbox.

Who can see your information

Your ID photo is visible only to you, the platform administrators (SCTL) and the administrator of your bonded terminal. Other records are visible to the people involved in the same job or terminal, according to their role.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database and sent over encrypted connections. Access is controlled by role, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information is kept while your account is active. If your sign-up request is rejected, the ID photo is removed automatically. You can ask us to correct or delete your account by contacting us below.
